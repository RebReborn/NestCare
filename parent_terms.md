## 2. The Booking Agreement & Contract Formation
Our platform uses a clear three-step flow to establish bookings:

```
[Parent Submits Request] ──> [Sitter Reviews Details] ──> [Sitter Accepts Request]
  (Holds authorized)            (Within 24 Hours)            (Contract Formed)
```

1.  **Submission**: You select a sitter, choose dates/hours, register children, and submit a booking request. The Platform places a temporary authorization hold on your credit card for the calculated total booking price.
2.  **Sitter Review**: The Sitter has up to 24 hours (or until 2 hours before the start time, whichever is earlier) to accept or decline the request.
3.  **Acceptance & Contract Formation**:
    > [!IMPORTANT]
    > A binding contract for care services is formed directly between you and the Sitter at the exact moment the Sitter accepts your booking request. The Platform is not a party to this care contract; the Platform acts solely as a marketplace facilitating connection, scheduling, and billing mediation.

## 3. Cancellation, Late-Arrival, and No-Show Rules

### Cancellation Rules
*   **Parent Cancellation**:
    *   **More than 24 Hours before start time**: You may cancel the booking with no penalty. The authorization hold on your payment card will be fully released.
    *   **Less than 24 Hours before start time**: You will be charged a late cancellation fee equivalent to **1 hour of care** at the sitter's hourly rate to compensate the provider for their reserved time.
*   **Sitter Cancellation**: If a sitter cancels a confirmed booking at any time, you will receive a **100% refund** (or the authorization hold will be immediately released).

### Late-Arrival Rules
*   **Parent Late Return**: If you arrive later than the scheduled checkout time, you will be billed for the additional time in **15-minute increments** based on the Sitter's hourly rate.
*   **Sitter Late Arrival**: If a sitter arrives late, the final invoice will be adjusted downward to reflect only the actual hours worked. Sitter late arrivals must be flagged in the care logs or via our dispute panel.

### No-Show Rules
*   **Sitter No-Show**: If a sitter fails to show up within 30 minutes of the scheduled start time without communication, the booking is declared a "No-Show." You will receive a **100% refund**, and the sitter's profile will be flagged for review and potential suspension.

## 5. Dispute Resolution & Complaints
In alignment with Canadian consumer protection guidance, we provide a structured, transparent mechanism to handle complaints:
*   **Filing a Dispute**: If you encounter an issue during a booking (e.g., sitter did not perform requested duties, arrived late, or engaged in unprofessional behavior), you can flag the booking and file a complaint via the **Support / Disputes** page.
*   **Mediation Process**: The Platform will investigate the complaint by reviewing chat histories, care logs, and interviewing both parties. We aim to resolve all disputes within **5 business days** of submission.
*   **Platform Decisions**: If the dispute is resolved in your favor, the Platform may issue a partial or full refund. The Platform's decisions regarding billing adjustments are final.
