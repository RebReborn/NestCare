# Childcare Provider / Sitter Agreement

**Last Updated: August 15, 2026**

This Childcare Provider / Sitter Agreement (the "Agreement") constitutes a legally binding agreement between you (the "Provider," "Sitter," or "you") and the childcare marketplace platform (the "Platform," "we," or "us"). By registering as a childcare provider on the Platform, you acknowledge, represent, and agree to follow all conditions outlined below.

---

## 1. Independent Contractor Relationship
*   **No Employment Relationship**: You explicitly acknowledge that you are registering as an independent contractor. You are not, and shall not represent yourself as, an employee, agent, partner, or joint venturer of the Platform.
*   **Freedom of Choice**: You maintain absolute discretion over your schedule, availability, hourly rates, service areas, and whether to accept or decline any specific booking requests. The Platform does not direct, supervise, or control the manner, details, or method of the childcare services you perform.
*   **No Benefits**: As an independent contractor, you are not eligible for any employment-related benefits, including but not limited to health insurance, paid time off, workers' compensation, or unemployment insurance from the Platform.

---

## 2. Eligibility & Age Requirements
*   **Minimum Age**: You must be at least 18 years of age to register and offer services as a childcare provider on this platform.
*   **Legal Work Status**: You represent and warrant that you are legally authorized to perform childcare services in the jurisdiction in which you offer them.
*   **Accurate Information**: You agree to provide true, accurate, and complete information during registration, including your full legal name, date of birth, address, and credentials.

---

## 3. Identity Verification & Background Check Requirements
*   **Identity Check**: You agree to undergo identity verification processes as requested by the Platform, which may include submitting government-issued identification cards or photos.
*   **Background Screening**: You must consent to and pass a criminal background check before receiving a verification badge on your profile. The Platform utilizes third-party screening services to run background checks.
*   **Limitation of Verification Badge**:
    > [!IMPORTANT]
    > The Platform does not guarantee that any provider is completely safe. The "Verified" badge displays only that a Sitter has completed the specific verification steps identified on their profile at the time the check was conducted. Parents are ultimately responsible for interviewing, checking references, and selecting the caregiver that fits their child's needs.

---

## 4. Qualifications & CPR/First-Aid Disclosures
*   **Truthful Disclosures**: You must honestly disclose all qualifications, certifications, educational history, and years of experience.
*   **CPR and First-Aid**: If you indicate on your profile that you possess a CPR or First-Aid certification, you must upload valid documentation verifying the certification. You agree to remove these designations from your profile immediately if your certification expires or is revoked.
*   **No Unsubstantiated Claims**: You shall not make any claims regarding special training, medical expertise, or license status that cannot be verified by official certificates.

---

## 5. Representations, Warranties, & Covenants
You represent, warrant, and covenant that:
*   You possess the necessary skills, experience, qualifications, and licenses (where applicable) to perform childcare services safely.
*   You have never been convicted of a felony, a crime involving violence, abuse, neglect, theft, or any offense involving children.
*   You do not have any physical or mental conditions, nor do you abuse substances, which could impair your ability to provide safe and reliable childcare services.

---

## 6. Code of Conduct & Prohibited Conduct
When executing care sessions booked through the Platform, you must adhere to the highest standard of professionalism.

### Code of Conduct
*   **Punctuality**: Arrive on time for all scheduled care sessions. Inform the parent immediately if you encounter unexpected delays.
*   **Active Supervision**: Keep children under active visual supervision at all times. Never leave a child unattended under any circumstances.
*   **Responsive Care**: Treat all children and family members with respect, patience, and dignity.

### Prohibited Conduct
*   **No Physical Discipline**: Under no circumstances shall you use physical discipline, corporal punishment, verbal abuse, or psychological threats of any kind on a child.
*   **No Substance Use**: You must never consume alcohol, marijuana, illegal drugs, or misuse prescription medications immediately prior to or during a care session. Smoking and vaping are strictly prohibited in the presence of children or inside the client's home.
*   **No Unauthorized Guests**: You must not bring guests, friends, family members, or pets to a booking unless the parent has given explicit, written approval beforehand.
*   **No Unauthorized Transportation**: You shall not transport children in a vehicle unless the parent has requested pickup/transportation as part of the booking parameters and provided appropriate child safety seats.

---

## 7. Child Safety Obligations & Reporting
*   **Mandatory Reporting**: If you suspect abuse, exploitation, or neglect of a child during a booking, you are obligated to contact the appropriate local family protective services or law enforcement agencies in compliance with mandatory reporting laws.
*   **Emergency Situations**: In the event of a medical emergency, you must immediately dial 911 (or local emergency numbers) to secure professional medical care, and then immediately notify the parents.

---

## 8. Confidentiality
*   **Privacy of Families**: You agree to keep all personal, medical, financial, and residential information regarding the families you serve strictly confidential.
*   **No Social Media Posting**: You must never post photographs, videos, names, or addresses of the children or parents you care for on social media or any public forum without the explicit, written consent of the parent.

---

## 9. Booking Cancellations
*   **Sitter Cancellation Policy**: Because parents rely on you for scheduled care, cancellations should be kept to a minimum. 
*   **Late Cancellations**: Cancelling a booking with less than 24 hours' notice without a documented emergency may result in a fee or temporary account suspension.
*   **Repeated Cancellations**: A pattern of frequent cancellations or failing to show up to scheduled bookings ("No-Shows") will result in permanent account termination.

---

## 10. Payment, Taxes, & Platform Fees
*   **Platform Payments**: All payments for care sessions booked through the Platform must be processed directly through the Platform's billing integrations. You agree not to solicit or accept cash or direct payments outside the platform ("Disintermediation").
*   **Platform Fees**: You agree that the Platform deducts a service fee (the platform percentage configuration) from the total booking amount before distributing payments to your account.
*   **Taxes**: You are solely responsible for calculating, reporting, and paying all applicable local, state, and federal taxes (including self-employment taxes) on the income you receive through the platform. The Platform will provide 1099 tax forms where required by law.

---

## 11. Dispute Resolution, Suspension, & Termination
*   **Dispute Mediation**: The Platform provides a Dispute Panel for parents and sitters to flag issues (e.g., payment discrepancies, cancellation fees). You agree to participate in good faith in any platform-led dispute resolution process.
*   **Account Suspension**: The Platform reserves the absolute right to temporarily suspend your account if a parent files a safety complaint, code of conduct violation report, or billing dispute, pending investigation.
*   **Termination**: The Platform may terminate your account and block access to the marketplace immediately and without prior notice for:
    *   Any violation of child safety obligations.
    *   Violating the Prohibited Conduct section (substance use, guests, physical discipline).
    *   Circumventing platform payments.
    *   Providing false credentials or failing identity verification checks.
